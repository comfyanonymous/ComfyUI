## Description

Part of the Blue Team Model series - decorates cars with Christmas tree LED lights. Creates festive vehicle scenes with colorful string lights wrapped around cars. Can be mixed with other car LoRAs for unique holiday vehicle combinations.

**Capabilities:**
- Christmas LED lights on cars
- Festive vehicle decorations
- Colorful string light effects
- Holiday car scenes
- Combines with other car LoRAs
