## Description

Modern, stylish, and comfortable open office space design. Industrial style with exposed pipes, blue ceilings, natural light, and minimalist aesthetics. Ideal for:
- Office design visualization
- Corporate interior scenes
- Tech/creative workspace backgrounds
- Professional settings
