## Usage Tips

### For Panty/Underwear Effects
- Combine panty LoRAs with anatomy LoRAs for better detail
- Use NSFW unlocking LoRAs (MysticXXX) for explicit content
- Lower CFG (1-4) works well for FLUX panty LoRAs

### For Stockings/Pantyhose
- Black pantyhose works best with full body shots
- Garter LoRAs benefit from view angle specifications (front/back/side)
- Lower strength (0.7-0.9) when combining multiple hosiery LoRAs

### For Clothing Effects
- See-through/sheer effects work at 0.7-1.0 strength
- Use "revealing" and "exposing" keywords for stronger effects
- Avoid word "wearing" with sheer clothes - use action verbs

### For Footwear
- Specify platform height for Pleaser shoes ("10inch platform base")
- Use realism LoRAs to avoid plastic look on heels
- Slingbacks need accurate prompting
