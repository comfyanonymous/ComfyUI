## Description

LoRA for generating "legs up" pose - woman lying down with legs raised in the air. Works well for top-down views and intimate scenes.

## Sample prompts

**Prompt 1 (Massage table):**
```
LegsUpPose, top down view. A beautiful 20 year old woman is lying down on a massage table. She is holding her knees to her chest and her toned legs are in the air.
```
