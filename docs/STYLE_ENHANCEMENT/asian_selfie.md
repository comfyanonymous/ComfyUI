## Description

Style LoRA trained on 150 Pinterest images of Asian selfies. Creates authentic amateur selfie aesthetic with:
- Low lighting / eerie atmosphere
- GoPro lens distortion
- Smeared/blurred background and foreground
- Low saturation
- Phone covering face effect
- Flash photography option
