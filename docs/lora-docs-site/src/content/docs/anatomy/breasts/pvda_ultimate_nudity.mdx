## Description

Exceptional amateur candid nude photographs LoRA for FLUX. Part of the PVDA (Ultimate Nudity) project aimed at creating realistic amateur nude photography. This version focuses specifically on **A-Cup breasts** with detailed areola and nipple control.

**Training Data:**
- 200 images of A Cup breasts
- 200 images of flat chests
- 100 images of B Cup breasts
- 150 images of women without makeup
- 150 images of women over 40 (to avoid "barely legal" bias)
- 100 images of vaginas/vulvas

**Technical:**
