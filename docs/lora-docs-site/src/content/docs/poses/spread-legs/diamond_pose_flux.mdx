## What is Diamond Pose?

The diamond pose features:
- **Legs:** Spread wide apart and raised in the air, forming a V or diamond shape
- **Feet:** Pointed/extended towards the camera
- **Vulva:** Prominently visible/centered in the image
- **Arms:** Usually relaxed or positioned naturally
- **Expression:** Confident, playful, or seductive

Works in various settings: outdoor, bedroom, furniture, vehicles.
